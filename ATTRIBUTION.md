# Attribution

## IQ Exact scoring implementation

The scoring methodology and reference code in this repository are published by **IQ Exact** ([iqexact.com](https://iqexact.com)) for transparency. They describe how IQ Exact computes fluid intelligence (Gf) scores in production.

## Open Matrices Item Bank (OMIB)

This scoring system uses **IRT calibration parameters** from the Open Matrices Item Bank (OMIB).

**Citation:**

> Koch, M., Spinath, F. M., Greiff, S., & Becker, N. (2022). Development and Validation of the Open Matrices Item Bank. *Journal of Intelligence*, 10(3), 41.  
> DOI: [10.3390/jintelligence10030041](https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence10030041)

**Item bank license:** GPLv3 (see original OMIB distribution and authors).

**What we publish:** item IDs, discrimination (`a`) and difficulty (`b`) parameters for Form A (22 items), and derived norm tables.

**What we do not publish:** answer keys, complete item rendering assets, or proprietary product code.

## Disclaimer

IQ Exact measures **fluid reasoning (Gf)** via figural matrices, not a full clinical IQ battery (e.g. WAIS). Norms v0 are anchored to the OMIB calibration sample (German university applicants, mean age ~19). Results are indicative estimates, not clinical diagnoses.
